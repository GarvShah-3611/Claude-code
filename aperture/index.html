The studio

Marta Oliveira founded Aperture in 2016, after eight years assisting in Paris.

She shoots medium format film alongside digital, and finishes every frame herself. That is the reason the studio takes twenty commissions a year and not more — the edit is the work, and it does not scale.

The studio sits above a framer’s workshop in Boavista, which is convenient, because most of what we make ends up on paper.
